Our Mission & Vision

The LeapOn Radon Initiative is a student-led public health campaign dedicated to raising awareness about the dangers of residential radon gas and its direct correlation to lung cancer.

The Challenge

Radon is an invisible, odorless, and tasteless radioactive gas that can leak into homes through foundational cracks. It is the second-leading cause of lung cancer in the United States, yet millions of homes remain untested.

Our Strategy

  • → Data Aggregation: Deploying community field teams to conduct radon level testing across states.
  • → Geospatial Analysis: Mapping field data to track high-risk zones in collaboration with academic and environmental health initiatives.
  • → Community Engagement: Hosting fundraisers and public awareness events.
